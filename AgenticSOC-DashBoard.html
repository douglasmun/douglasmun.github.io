AgenticSOC — Autonomous Security Operations Intelligence
AgenticSOC is a tier-1 agentic security operations platform that fuses Extreme Value Theory (EVT/GPD), Bayesian belief networks, topology-aware risk propagation, and mission-graph operability modelling into a single continuously-updated fused risk score. The system proposes, justifies, and requests human approval for containment actions — providing a full cryptographic audit trail for every decision.

AGENTICLLM-powered reasoning engine
HCILHuman-in-the-loop approvals
AUDITImmutable decision ledger
Architecture Layers
Extreme Value Theory — GPD
Anomaly signals exceeding threshold u are modelled as a Generalised Pareto Distribution. The exceedance probability P(X > x | X > u) = [1 + ξ(x−u)/σ]^{−1/ξ} provides rigorous tail risk quantification. Current fit: ξ=0.41, σ=2.31, u=47.2, 23 exceedances from 312 observations.
Bayesian Inference Engine
Each observed signal E_i provides likelihood ratio LR_i = P(E_i|H)/P(E_i|¬H). Joint update: P(H|E) = Λ·P(H) / [Λ·P(H) + (1−P(H))]. Current posterior for Lateral Movement: 0.763, driven by LR product 82.4× across 6 independent signals.
Decision Fusion — Weighted Sum
Fused risk R_fused = Σ w_i·s_i integrates 8 orthogonal dimensions: scenario match (0.20), topology (0.15), tail EVT (0.15), impact (0.10), recovery (0.10), mission (0.10), scenario phase (0.10), temporal recency (0.10). Weights sum to 1.0 and are recalibrated quarterly.
Mission Graph Operability
Capability operability O(C) = min{O(a) : a ∈ deps(C)} for critical path assets. Asset operability: O(a) = 1 − R_fused(a). Mission breach declared when O(C) falls below threshold θ_C = 0.70. Currently 3 of 5 capabilities in breach state.
